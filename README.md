A factual reference on compatible building bricks for adult collectors, maintained by [BrickAlternative](https://brickalternative.com/).

## 1. Clutch, Alignment & Color Sampling

Before listing a batch, bricks are sampled for clutch (how elements connect and hold) and alignment (how plates and tiles sit together). Tiles are compared against a reference palette under daylight-balanced lighting rather than relying on uncalibrated screens.

- Read more: [About & Quality Checks](https://brickalternative.com/about/#quality)
- Deep dive: [What Compatible Building Bricks Mean Here](https://brickalternative.com/blog/compatible-building-bricks/)

## 2. Planning Mixed Builds

When combining third-party sets with existing parts:

1. **Test load-bearing joints first:** Dry-fit a short run of plates and Technic connections before committing to a full modular floor.
2. **Follow the numbered bag sequence:** Build the primary skeleton from the set's own bags before introducing older elements.
3. **Inspect under neutral light:** Compare neighboring tiles under daylight-balanced lighting before permanent placement.

## 3. Missing-Piece Replacement

If a bag is short a piece, record the set title, build step, and part shown in the manual, and follow the [Missing Pieces & Brick Quality Guide](https://brickalternative.com/blog/missing-pieces-and-brick-quality/) for free replacement.
